# PCBWay Boards & Stencil
[PCBWay](https://pcbway.com) generously sponsored a run of PCBs and even allowed me to select some premium options, such as 2 ounce copper layers.
Here are a couple of photos of the excellent PCBWay products I received free of charge and a photo of one of the boards as fully assembled by me (Matt).

![Photo of front of PCBWay PCB](pcbway-board-front.jpg)

Take note of the excellent silkscreen -- I didn't put that dingo sketch on there for no reason.  All the fine detail from the design was preserved and faithfully reproduced.  The soldermask feels even more silky-smooth than it looks, while remaining durable and scratch resistant.  The simple HASL surface treatment that I selected was remarkably flat and smooth for HASL.  As you can see below, it supported paste reflow & hand soldering well.

![Photo of PCBWay Steel Solder Paste Stencil](pcbway-stencil-front.jpg)

I asked PCBWay's representative if I could also get a solder paste stencil to show how easy populating a board with the benefit of one of their stencils can be.  I'll be posting a video of that, but for now look at the fine details and smooth, flawless opening cuts in this nicely electropolished steel stencil.  Functionally, the stencil performed very well; solder paste release was clean and even.

![Photo of their board that I hand populated](hand-assembled-by-matt.jpg)

I'll ultimately be posting a video of the assembly process, particularly demonstrating how the solder paste stencil can be such a benefit when working with fine-pitch components such as some of those in this design.  In the meantime, here's a photo of the finished board, hand populated.  I wish to emphasize that this assembly was my own work, and not from the assembly service at PCBWay.  Any flaws in the placement or soldering are my own.
